RULE (hard)
  For each leaf in t03, t04:
    Count of I=1 on that leaf's FA open windows must be EXACTLY 2.

  Evidence — k-ones (first k opens):
    k=1  → Y never rises
    k=2  → Y sticks through end (PASS)
    k≥3  → Y rises then dies (B clears; FAIL)

  Evidence — spacing / which opens (does NOT relax exactly-2):
    Two ones on opens[0] & opens[1+g], g=0..4
      → always PASS (open-window gaps between the two ones do not matter)
    Two ones on opens[s] & opens[s+1+g], s=1..3, g=0..4
      → always PASS (first one need not be the first open)
    Three ones on opens[0], opens[step], opens[2*step], step=1+g, g=0..4
      → always FAIL (third open-window one kills; spacing does not save it)

  Gate: Y = A ∧ B
